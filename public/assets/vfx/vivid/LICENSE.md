# Vivid Motion VFX

The sprite strips in this directory are derived from two free Vivid Motion asset packs:

- **Vivid Motion: Combat Arcana Vol. 1**
  https://vivid-motion-assets.itch.io/vivid-motion-combat-arcana-vol-1
- **Vivid Motion: Elemental Arcana Vol. 1**
  https://vivid-motion-assets.itch.io/vivid-motion-elemental-arcana-vol-1

Both source pages permit commercial and personal use and modification. Raw sprite sheets may not be resold or redistributed as standalone asset packs.

The source grid sheets were rearranged into horizontal frame strips for CSS animation. Included derivatives cover Sonic, Thunder, Void, Arcane, Holy, Nature, Lightning, Fire, and Wind effects.

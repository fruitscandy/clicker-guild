# Weapon attack audio sources

The `.ogg` files in this directory are renamed, unmodified source recordings from these Kenney packs:

- [RPG Audio](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio): `knifeSlice.ogg`, `knifeSlice2.ogg`
- [Impact Sounds](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds): selected `impactMetal_light`, `impactMetal_medium`, `impactMetal_heavy`, `impactPlate_heavy`, and `impactPunch_heavy` recordings

Both source packs are released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**. Attribution is not required. This file is retained to document provenance and license status.

The game combines the recordings at runtime with gain, pitch, filtering, stereo placement, controlled reverb, transient reinforcement, and sub-impact layers. The processed mix is designed specifically for the 15-step weapon progression in Clicker Guild.

character against the 15 equipped weapon tiers. All listed submissions are
released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**:

- Tiers 1–7: [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) by artisticdude

Attribution is not required. These source links are retained as provenance and
license evidence. Inclusion in this audition does not mean the samples have
been selected for the final game.

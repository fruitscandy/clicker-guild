## Flow Music battle rotation

The battle soundtrack also includes four instrumental tracks generated by the
project owner with Google Flow Music. Normal battles rotate between the first
two tracks and boss battles rotate between the final two tracks.

| File | Scene | BPM | Length | Source |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `flow-candidates/vanguards-charge.m4a` | Normal battle | 110 | 171.95s | [Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/b37e9a5f-132c-415a-841c-3a3e29526ef9) |
| `flow-candidates/iron-advance.m4a` | Normal battle | 110 | 177.00s | [Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/2485aff6-a9b2-4939-8c33-28c90e6d8f91) |
| `flow-candidates/fantasy-boss-battle.m4a` | Boss battle | 140 | 179.65s | [Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/dcdb6ecf-6a5d-4d18-b719-045646eae3d2) |
| `flow-candidates/fantasy-boss-battle-take-2.m4a` | Boss battle | 140 | 172.33s | [Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/6560582a-52ed-40eb-b7c5-834f9f8ed4e6) |

The source links and generation prompts are retained here for provenance. These
tracks are looped by the browser player; they are not claimed to be sample-exact
seamless loops.

The source generator is `scripts/generate-bgm.mjs`. It uses deterministic additive synthesis and percussion synthesis, so the checked-in WAV files can be regenerated without external dependencies.
